## Executive Summary

**Verdict:** ✅ **VALIDATED** - Strong market fit with clear differentiation

**Key Findings:**
1. **Blocking is broken** - All competitors have ineffective app blocking; TimeBeam's timer-based approach fills this gap
2. **Mobile/desktop sync is broken** - RescueTime doesn't aggregate mobile data; TimeBeam's real-time sync solves this
3. **Pricing sweet spot confirmed** - $7/month for Pro tier aligns with market expectations
4. **Product Hunt + SEO works** - $500-$2,000 to acquire first 1,000 users

**Recommendation:** Proceed to V1 with focus on **effective blocking** and **cross-platform sync**

## Research 1: Competitor User Reviews

### RescueTime (Trustpilot: 2.9/5)
**What users love:**
- Actionable data insights
- Long-term data retention
- Easy setup

**What users hate:**
- UI complexity
- Blocking ineffectiveness ("doesn't work")
- Misleading free trial (14-day only)

**Top missing feature:** Working app blocking (iOS/Android)
